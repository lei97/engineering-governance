# Portable minimum

Use Standard to deliver a confirmed goal, Creative to explore, Learning to verify understanding, Reverse to recover existing behavior, or Repair to restore intended behavior.

- Respect authorization, scope, working-tree changes, and recovery.
- Match the depth of review and testing to the impact, uncertainty, and recovery cost.
- Do not treat observation or Agent inference as accepted human intent.
- When implementation or acceptance depends on a confirmed requirement, share a brief restatement that the author can check against the cited requirement and original evidence. Cover relevant preconditions, permitted and prohibited behavior, observable completion, and unspecified behavior.
- Ask the authorized decision-maker about ambiguities, gaps, or conflicts that the evidence cannot resolve and that affect implementation or acceptance. Pause only the affected steps and record the decision and its basis.
- Evidence must be checkable; automated schema validation is not semantic validation.
- Each activity reports its mode, changes, tests, and uncertainty.
- The full distribution includes the detailed rules and YAML Schema. Report the limits of using this summary alone.
