# Standard mode

Confirm the authorized objective and acceptance criteria before changing formal artifacts. Read the Standard entry in `specifications/modes.md` at the resolved governance root.

When implementation or acceptance depends on interpreting a confirmed requirement, follow **需求理解核对** in `specifications/change-protocol.md` at that root. Use [the portable minimum](governance-summary.md) when the specifications are unavailable.

Keep the change within the confirmed scope. Verify it against the requirements and report the checks, failures, unverified behavior, and any decisions still needed.
